## What I do

I'm a **fractional COO** in Pittsburgh who builds the operating layer founder-led and expert-led businesses have outgrown. Fifteen years of the same job at bigger and bigger scale — community and program operations at the U.S. Green Building Council, workforce and platform operations at the International WELL Building Institute, and fractional ops, marketing ops, and revenue ops since.

The job is always some version of the same move: **map how the business actually works**, get the standards and decisions free of the few people who carry them, and build **the operating layer the whole team can run on**.
